FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How much does a project cost? +

The cost depends on complexity, features, integrations, timeline, and documentation needs. After reviewing your requirements, we can discuss a proposed scope and estimate before work begins.

Can you help improve an existing project? +

Yes. Depending on the project's condition and agreed scope, support may include debugging, interface improvements, feature implementation, testing, or setup guidance.

What should I prepare before contacting you? +

If available, share your project title, problem statement, intended users, required features, preferred technology, deadline, and school or client requirements. It's okay if some details are still undecided.

Can I contact you if I only have an idea? +

Yes. Describe the problem you want to solve and who would use the solution. We can discuss the possible approach and clarify the requirements before deciding on a scope.

What happens after I submit the form? +

The form opens your email application with a prepared inquiry. Review the message and send it yourself. The website does not automatically send or store your inquiry.
